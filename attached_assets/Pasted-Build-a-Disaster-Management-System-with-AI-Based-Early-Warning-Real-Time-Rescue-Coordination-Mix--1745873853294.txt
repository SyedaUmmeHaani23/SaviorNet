3. Presentation Strategy (🔥 THIS is VERY IMPORTANT)
When you present to the judges, start with an emotional, powerful story.

Example:
"Imagine you are in a flood-hit village. Roads are cut off. You don't know where to go. No rescue in sight.
Now imagine — you get an alert 1 hour earlier. You press a button. A rescue boat is on its way! This is the power of SaviorNet."

Then explain:

Problem: Disaster casualties because of late response + poor communication.

Solution: SaviorNet = Early prediction + Fast rescue.

Tech: "We use real-time data + AI prediction + mobile app + rescue coordination."

Impact: "Our system can save thousands of lives and millions in property damages."

Future Scope: "It can be expanded to national disaster response forces and NGOs."
